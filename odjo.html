Preface

Everything, from one place

Odjo is a web app that answers a business’s calls, emails, WhatsApps and web forms, works out what each person needs, and turns it into a case on the right record. Lots of channels, one place they all come back to.

Our first logo didn’t say any of that. So I redrew it around the idea that runs through the product: many things, connected, growing from a single root.

Part one

The idea

Ideation

London’s biggest forest

Before drawing anything, we went looking for where the brand should come from. We grew up near Epping Forest, which runs from the edge of east London up into Essex: an ancient forest, kept open to everyone.

A forest fitted Odjo twice over. It ties the brand to London, and it’s about growth: something new that keeps developing, with everything connected underneath.

Area

About 6,100 acres, or roughly 17 Hyde Parks.

Length

End to end, north to south, and never more than about two and a half miles wide.

Part two

The mark

App-icon size. A search icon reads as “search”; the tree reads as Odjo.

Fig. 1 — The old mark

  1. 01

    The old mark

    Our first logo was a blue magnifying glass with a dot in the middle, made on Canva by my brother. But it was a search icon, and search icons are everywhere. It said nothing about what Odjo does or who’s behind it.

  2. 02

    Look at the room

    Most AI products lean the same way: black and white, sharp geometry, a bit industrial. It looks like the future, and it all looks the same. Odjo talks to people on a business’s behalf, so I wanted it to feel warmer than that.

  3. 03

    Go organic

    So instead of black and white, the forest. Its outline is where the mark starts, and its green is the palette: one deep forest green, and white.

  4. 04

    Draw one leaf

    One leaf, drawn as a single line on a guide circle, with its tail crossing over itself at the base.

  5. 05

    Turn it around one centre

    Five copies, turned around the same point. Rotational symmetry is a habit of mine; it’s in the KCL BSOC shapla and the King’s MedTech helix too. It makes a mark feel balanced from any angle.

  6. 06

    Join every leaf

    The leaves don’t float apart. Each one’s line runs on into the next, so the whole crown is connected, and two of them meet to become a single stem. Many things, growing from one place.

  7. 07

    Every channel, one root

    That’s the product. Calls, WhatsApps, emails, web forms and messages all come in on different branches, and Odjo ties them back to one place: the right record for the right person.

  8. 08

    Test it small

    It’s a single weight of line on a flat colour, so it holds up as an app icon, a favicon and a profile picture.
